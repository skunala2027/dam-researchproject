Welcome to my Project! My name is Saketh Kunala, a student from Andover passionate about applying my Computer Science knowledge to solve and analyze real-world environmental issues. As an APES student, I dedicated my summer to pursue a project to analyze the restoration efforts along the local Shawsheen River in Andover using the data-driven tools of SQL, Artificial Intelligence, and more. Our dataset was collected by dedicated volunteers at the Balmoral and Marland Place dams, where they hand-counted the number of fish present throughout the riverway. However, considering that there could be human error, it's important to acknowledge that these results won't be 100% accurate.

Prior to 2017, these dams were blocking migratory pathways for local fish, most notably the River Herring. These human-made dams that hold large quantities of water also pose problems to infrastructure as they are prone to frequent flooding.

I hope that my project inspires and informs others interested in environmental conservation. If you have any questions about my methodology or are interested in learning more, please don't hesitate to reach out!
