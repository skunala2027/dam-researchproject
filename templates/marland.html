Reasons why the Marland Place dam was taken down:

  1. Costly to maintain
  2. Had no purpose for the Town of Andover; stopped providing electricity to the local mills
  3. Negatively impacted the local ecosystem in the Shawsheen River, especially blocking the fish from their migratory routes
  4. Posed a high risk of floods to the nearby buildings and shops
