Results of the Balmoral Dam being removed:

  1. Restored miles upon miles of natural habitat for fish!
    • Over 4.1 miles of the Shawsheen River for recreational purposes and 16 acres of habitat for fish were opened.
  2. Water is now regularly flowing without any disruptions, thus resulting in lowered flood risks.
  3. Many fish species like the River Herring have returned to the Shawsheen River and are prospering greatly.
  4. More opportunities for outdoor recreational activities like sightseeing, swimming, and more!
  5. The Town of Andover and local property owners in Andover don't have to spend any extra money to maintain the obsolete dam structures.
